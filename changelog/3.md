- Images: the paintCompression "none" keeps the pixels a script gives an image (its imageData, alphaData or maskData, a crop) uncompressed, and draws them as they are, until their data is needed: when the stack is saved, the image closes or is copied, or its text, size or paintCompression is read, they are compressed as "rle" compresses them, and from then on the image is what rle makes of it (until then, a pixel its maskData hides keeps its colour, which rle does not). Setting the imageData of a 640 x 480 image took about 34 ms with transparency (2 ms without) and takes about 1 (0.4). The other values of the paintCompression are unchanged (#3)
